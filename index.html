What We Do

Gathering Around
India's Great Traditions

From the burst of colors at Holi to the glow of a thousand diyas at Diwali, we bring our community together through India's sacred and joyful calendar of celebrations.

🎉
Festival Gatherings

We organize celebrations for India's most beloved holidays — bringing food, music, dance, and community together throughout the year.

🙏
Religious Observances

We honor India's spiritual heritage through puja gatherings, bhajan evenings, and reverent observances of sacred occasions.

🏛️
Temple Stall & Fundraising

Every year we set up our stall at the local temple, raising over $5,000 annually through handmade goods, food, and cultural offerings.
