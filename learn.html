About Telugu

The "Italian of the East"

Telugu is one of India's oldest classical languages, spoken by over 80 million people across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. Its script is uniquely melodic: every letter ends in a vowel sound, giving the language its famously flowing, musical quality. Whether you're reconnecting with your roots or learning for the first time, these free lessons are for you.

Did You Know?

Telugu is the only Indian language where every single letter ends in a vowel sound, which is why linguists nicknamed it the "Italian of the East." It has been a classical language since 2008.
