Our Story

Born from Pride
in our Heritage

The Telangana Initiative was founded by a group of high school students who shared a simple belief: that the rich culture, language, and spiritual traditions of Telangana deserve to be celebrated, shared, and passed on to the next generation.

· · ·

Over four years, we have grown from an informal gathering into an active youth nonprofit — organizing festivals, religious observances, and an annual fundraising stall at our local temple that raises over $5,000 each year for community programs.

· · ·

"We wanted a place where second-generation Telugu youth could feel proud of where they come from — and where anyone curious about our culture could join in."

Our Journey

Four Years of
Growing Together

Year 1 — Freshman Year

Founded the Telangana Initiative with a small group of friends. Hosted our first Diwali gathering and participated in the temple cultural fair for the first time, raising just over $1,000.

Year 2 — Sophomore Year

Expanded our festival calendar to include Ugadi, Holi, and Bathukamma. Formalized our temple stall operation and raised $3,500 — tripling our first year.

Year 3 — Junior Year

Launched informal Telugu language sessions for community members. Temple stall revenue crossed $5,000 for the first time. Began building this website and expanding our online presence.

Year 4 — Senior Year

Launched free interactive Telugu lessons online. Continued all major festivals. Raised over $5,000 again at the temple stall. Passed the torch to the next generation of student leaders.
